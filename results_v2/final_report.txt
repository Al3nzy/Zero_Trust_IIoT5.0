10. BOTTOM LINE
====================================================================================================
  * Best overall detector: LightGBM (class-aware MI) (Macro-F1 0.634+/-0.024, accuracy 0.776+/-0.019); best rare-class (U2R) recall: CNN only (0.642+/-0.015).
  * Unseen attack types are the main weakness of supervised models; the novelty fusion raises unseen-type detection to 0.65 (LightGBM (global MI)) at +0.3 points of benign false alarms and +0.4 points of seen-type detection.
  * Trust: the original confidence-only rule detects 0% of compromised devices; the class-aware CUSUM rule detects 100% (median 2 rounds), with 0.0% false quarantines on a heterogeneous fleet using per-device baselines; the proved bounds held empirically: True.
  * Ledger: mean tamper detection hash chain only 17%, + signatures 67%, + signatures + anchors 91% (signatures + external anchors needed; it is an audit log, not a blockchain).
  * Privacy: formal DP-SGD (eps 1-8) lowers Macro-F1 from 0.62 to about 0.45 and U2R recall from 0.34 to 0.00: report it as a privacy-utility trade-off.
  * NOT YET ESTABLISHED: generalisation beyond NSL-KDD (no additional dataset has been run: use --unsw-train/--unsw-test/--edge); trust results are a replay simulation, not a deployment.
