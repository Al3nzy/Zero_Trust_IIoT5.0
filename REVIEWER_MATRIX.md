## What the evidence does NOT support (say this in the paper)
* The deep model is not better than gradient boosting; the contribution is the framework, not the BiLSTM.
* Accuracy is about 0.77 on the overlap-free official split; R2L recall stays near 0.2 because the test R2L flows differ from the training R2L flows (guess_passwd: telnet/RSTO in training, pop_3/SF with successful logins in test).
* The hybrid LightGBM + BiLSTM ensemble gave no gain over LightGBM (Macro-F1 0.636 vs 0.634; with novelty fusion 0.645 vs 0.667).
* Trust results are a replay simulation, not a deployment. Novelty-detector ranking was inspected on NSL-KDD test data and needs external confirmation.
